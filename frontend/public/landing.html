Less scattered.
More manageable.

Assignments, readings, and revision can pile up quickly. StudyFlow brings them together so you can decide what needs your attention next.

Choose when you want to work, keep real deadlines separate, and revisit unfinished tasks each morning. Your plan can change with your day.

Main features

Small tools for a steadier routine.

01 / CAPTURE

A place for every task

Add work to your inbox and organize it by subject. Get it out of your head before deciding when to do it.

02 / PLAN

A flexible week

See today, the week, or your calendar. Move tasks to another day while keeping their deadlines intact.

03 / REFLECT

Progress you can see

Review unfinished work with a morning check-in, record study sessions, and look back at your progress.

How it works

Start with one manageable day.

1. Gather your work

Capture tasks and group them by subject.

2. Pick your focus

Schedule what you want to work on today.

3. Check in and adjust

Mark tasks complete and make room for what comes next.

The project

Simple by design.

StudyFlow is a personal study planner built to run locally, with a SQLite database and JSON data exports.

Try StudyFlow →GitHub ↗Project documentation ↗
What is it built with?

React and TypeScript for the interface, Express for the API, and Prisma with SQLite for storage.
